# TIGERFX ACADEMY — Innovation & Design Report

**Deliverable 13 of the $10,000 Web Development Challenge.**
This report explains what we built beyond the brief, why we built it that way, and how each decision serves the educational mission: *zero knowledge → independent trading mastery.*

---

## 1. Product thesis

Most trading education fails beginners in one of three ways: walls of text with static images, fake-interactive apps that reward clicking rather than understanding, or signal-selling funnels disguised as courses. TIGERFX Academy was designed against all three:

1. **See it → Try it → Get coached.** Every concept is introduced by an animated chart story before any technical wording appears. Every lesson ends in practice. Every mistake produces a targeted explanation.
2. **Honesty as a feature.** Risk warnings appear where they matter (inside lessons, inside tools), "liquidity" is explicitly framed as a behavioural model (not visibility into institutional orders), replay is strictly past-only, and completion promises *demonstrated knowledge*, never profit.
3. **Bilingual parity.** Bahasa Melayu is not a translation afterthought — every lesson, quiz explanation, widget, achievement and tooltip carries equal-quality BM text, written idiomatically for the Malaysian trading community (English trading terms retained where BM speakers actually use them).

## 2. Innovations beyond the brief

### 2.1 A bespoke canvas chart engine (not a library)

We rejected TradingView Lightweight Charts and similar libraries and wrote `lib/chart/engine.ts` (~900 lines) because the brief's hardest requirements live *inside* the chart:

- **Honest replay.** The engine renders only up to a `visibleTo` index. Future candles are not merely hidden — they do not exist in the render pass, making hindsight leaks structurally impossible.
- **Lesson overlays.** Scripted teaching scenes (highlight bands, pulsing markers, caption-driven candle reveals) share the exact same renderer as free practice — what students see in lessons is what they use in the simulator.
- **Drawing intelligence.** Full hit-testing, selection, move, undo/redo, and a three-click trade tool that renders live risk/reward boxes with R-multiples — the same tool used to grade Arena trade plans.
- **Zero dependencies, zero licence risk, ~0 ms chart load time.**

### 2.2 Rule-based drawing feedback ("explain my analysis")

The single most differentiated feature: **students draw on the chart and receive specific structural feedback about what they drew.**

- Horizontal lines are checked against fractal swing detection: *"Strong level — price reacted here 3 times (equal highs — a liquidity pool above the market)."*
- Trendlines are fitted against swing sequences; zones are scored on reaction count, tightness and FVG overlap; trade plans are evaluated for structural stop placement, side correctness and real R:R.

This is computed analysis of the visible candles — **not a fake AI**. The brief forbade simulated AI, so we built genuine deterministic analysis and wrote feedback copy around what it can honestly claim. The same evaluators grade Practice Arena submissions, which means the grading logic is inspectable and testable.

### 2.3 The Scenario Composer (deterministic narrative market data)

All teaching charts are generated by composable, seeded builders (`injectSweep`, `injectEqualHighs`, `injectFVG`, `injectBreak`, `makeRange`, `makeTrend`…). This gives:

- **Narrative control** — a lesson can say "the sweep happens at candle 25" and it always does;
- **Perfect reproducibility** across devices and languages;
- **Zero market-data licensing issues** and honest "simulated data" labelling.

Scenario charts in the Arena are built by *story templates* (sweep-short, sweep-long, failed-setup…), so the educational situation is guaranteed — no random chart can accidentally teach the wrong thing.

### 2.4 Guided Task System in the simulator

The simulator's guided mode derives its tasks **from the actual chart data** (find the liquidity pool → mark it with a line; tag the sweep candle; draw the FVG zone; plan a trade), grades each attempt with the analysis engine, awards XP, and advances. It works on any generated market — an endless, self-grading practice loop. Independent mode removes all hand-holding for free practice.

### 2.5 The five-beat sweep sequence

The brief's liquidity-sweep teaching example became a full lesson (`l3-sweeps`) split across two animated scenes (pool formation → trap & displacement), a clickable "find the sweep candle" task with targeted miss feedback, a written sweep-vs-continuation distinction ("the close and the follow-through decide"), and an Arena scenario pair that contrasts a *valid* sweep against an *invalid* one (close above + follow-through = breakout continuation). Students learn the boundary of the concept, not just its happy path.

### 2.6 Mistake-diagnosis pedagogy

Two mechanisms specifically target the "why did I lose?" gap:

- **Per-option quiz explanations.** Every wrong option has its own coaching text explaining *that specific misconception* ("the wick is a question; the next candles answer it").
- **Failure-diagnosis scenario** (`sc-failed-setup`): a correctly-planned losing trade where the correct answer is *"the loss was a planned cost — judge the decision, not the result."* We teach the difference between process and outcome explicitly, which most beginner content ignores entirely.

### 2.7 NO TRADE as a first-class answer

Multiple quizzes and scenarios reward *not trading* ("wait for confirmation", "stand aside through news"). The glossary contains NO TRADE as a defined term. A trading academy that only rewards entries teaches gambling; ours grades discipline.

### 2.8 Live session clock with IANA-correct DST

Session times are computed from `Intl.DateTimeFormat` against `Europe/London` / `America/New_York` / `Asia/Tokyo` and displayed in **Malaysia Time (GMT+8)** — so daylight-saving transitions are handled by the platform's tz database rather than hard-coded tables. The London–NY overlap is called out live when active.

### 2.9 Local-first progress with export/import

No signup wall — the brief demanded instant learning. XP, lesson steps, quiz scores, achievements, streaks and history persist via `localStorage` behind a pub/sub store (`useSyncExternalStore`), with JSON export/import for backup or device transfer. The store API is backend-ready if accounts are added later.

### 2.10 Minor delights

- Animated hero chart that tells the sweep+FVG story on loop (landing page).
- Candle Anatomy Lab: sliders reshape a live candle and the lab *names what you built* (doji, hammer, marubozu…) with meaning.
- Flip-card bilingual glossary with search and category filters.
- Achievement toasts with real criteria; rank progression (Cub → Market Master).
- Structure Builder drill: click the swings, get graded against fractal pivot detection.

## 3. Design system

- **Identity:** deep midnight surfaces (`#05080F → #152038`) with a gold trading accent (`#F5B942`), emerald `#22C98B` bull / rose `#FF5C7A` bear palette, glassmorphic cards, radial gold glows.
- **Typography:** Space Grotesk (display) + Inter (body), self-hosted at build time via `next/font` — no runtime font requests, perfect language glyph coverage.
- **Motion:** purposeful only — candle reveals, step fades, scroll reveals, hover lifts; `prefers-reduced-motion` fully honoured.
- **Charts:** bespoke canvas styling with rounded bodies, glow on the active candle, chip-style price tags — deliberately closer to a professional terminal than to a toy.

## 4. Educational integrity audit (against brief §6)

| Requirement | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Trading risks explained clearly | Risk callouts inside Level 1 lessons, sizing lesson, tools page, footer, broker page |
| No profit promises | Completion = "demonstrated educational competence, not guaranteed skill or profitability" — stated in lesson 1 and the graduation lesson |
| Example trades labelled simulated | Every chart carries "Simulated teaching data" labels; hero chart labelled too |
| Liquidity not presented as order-book visibility | Explicit honesty callout in `l3-liquidity` and the glossary entry |
| No future information in replay | Engine-level `visibleTo` cut-off; analysis runs on revealed candles only |
| Risk calculators state assumptions | Assumptions panel inside the calculator (100 oz/lot, no spread/commission/slippage, floor-rounded lots) |
| Broker contract-size awareness | Taught in the lots lesson and repeated in tool assumptions |
| Prerequisites enforced softly | Linear curriculum order recommended, nothing hard-locked ("learn at your pace") |
| When NOT to trade | NO TRADE answers rewarded; news discipline; failure-diagnosis scenario |
